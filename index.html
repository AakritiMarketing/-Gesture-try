How to set it up: stand the phone/tablet between the two of you, screen facing the deaf or mute person (tap "Flip screen" once it's turned around so it reads right-side-up for them). The front camera faces the same way as the screen, so it watches their hands while the sign panel shows them what you're saying. The speaker plays audio for you on the other side.

Real limits, plainly: the sign-recognition side matches ~7 static hand shapes held briefly - it can't follow fluent signing at full speed, because that needs a model trained on motion over time, not single frozen poses. The speech side matches spoken words against a fixed list (~35 words) - anything outside that list shows as plain text, not a sign. Treat this as a working starting point to build on, not a finished interpreter.
